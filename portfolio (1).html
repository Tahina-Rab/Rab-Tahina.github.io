Automatic Elevation & Azimuth Positioning System

  • Context: End-of-study project at EAMAC (Electronics & Computer Science): a real prototype, built from design to working device.
  • Problem: Setting a surface to a precise elevation and azimuth angle by hand is slow and imprecise; the goal was to automate it and control it remotely.
